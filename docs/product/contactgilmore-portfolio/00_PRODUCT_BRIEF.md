# Product Brief

Status: active product support document  
Positioning checkpoint: 2026-08-08

The site is Mike Gilmore's professional portfolio and technical writing platform. Its job is not to duplicate a resume. It should quickly establish a coherent professional identity, show evidence of how Mike solves technical/customer problems, and provide deeper proof through case studies, engineering projects, and writing.

## Recommended positioning architecture

Primary identity:

```text
Technical Delivery & Customer Solutions
```

This is a message architecture, not yet final hero copy. It intentionally contains the strongest overlapping role families without forcing the site to choose one narrow job title.

Supporting role territory:

```text
Implementation / Professional Services
Technical Success
Solutions / Customer Engineering
Technical Program or Systems Delivery
```

Supporting technical credibility:

```text
SaaS integrations
REST APIs and authentication
SQL and data troubleshooting
cloud and automation
observability and reliability
technical documentation and writing
```

Cloud/SRE depth supports the main identity; it should not compete with it in the hero.

## Three proof pillars

### 1. Implementation and Technical Delivery

Requirements, onboarding/implementation coordination, UAT/testing, release readiness, training/documentation, and cross-functional execution.

### 2. Integrations and Troubleshooting

APIs, authentication/tokens, SQL/data validation, middleware/integration patterns, identity/access considerations, and structured root-cause troubleshooting.

### 3. Customer and Stakeholder Leadership

Discovery, technical translation, expectation setting, escalation handling, partner/internal coordination, presentations, and durable documentation.

## Hero-message hierarchy

The eventual first viewport should communicate in this order:

```text
Mike Gilmore
-> coherent professional identity
-> one concise value/outcome statement
-> small supporting technical/delivery descriptor
-> primary CTA to Work
-> secondary CTA to Resume or Writing
```

Do not use a hero constructed from four or more competing titles.

## Content demotion/removal from current homepage

Demote or remove from the homepage:

- long introductory biography before proof;
- separate Sales Engineering vs Reliability Engineering skill inventories;
- exhaustive cloud/service lists;
- framework/build-tool discussion in the hero;
- repeated claims that are not tied to evidence;
- `Cloud Architect | SRE | Customer Sales and Support Leader` as the primary identity.

Those capabilities may still appear where context supports them.

## Success definition

A visitor should understand Mike's professional value in under a minute, see evidence rather than only skill claims, and be able to choose a deeper path without reading the entire homepage.

Final wording remains Mike's owner decision.
