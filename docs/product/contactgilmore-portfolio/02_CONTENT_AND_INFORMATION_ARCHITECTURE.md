# Content and Information Architecture

Target top-level structure:

```text
Home
Work / Case Studies
Projects
Writing / Blog
About / Experience
Resume
Contact links
```

Homepage target sections:

1. concise hero and role/value proposition;
2. three professional capability/proof pillars;
3. selected professional case studies;
4. smaller technical-project section;
5. three featured articles;
6. compact experience/credentials;
7. short personal/about statement and contact CTA.

Avoid a complete resume dump, giant technology icon wall, skill meters, or every article on the homepage.
