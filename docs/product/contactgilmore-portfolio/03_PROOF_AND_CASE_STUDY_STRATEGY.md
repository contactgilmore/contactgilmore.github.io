# Proof and Case Study Strategy

Primary proof should shift from basic technology demonstrations to sanitized professional case studies.

Candidate case-study lanes:

1. Enterprise SaaS integrations and API/authentication troubleshooting.
2. Customer implementation / technical delivery / partner coordination.
3. Reliability, observability, incident, or operational improvement.

Each case study should state problem/context, Mike's role, constraints, approach, technologies/patterns, decisions/tradeoffs, outcome where safely shareable, and lessons.

Existing Terraform/New Relic projects remain useful as secondary engineering evidence.
