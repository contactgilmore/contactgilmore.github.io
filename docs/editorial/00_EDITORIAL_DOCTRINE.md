# Portfolio Editorial Doctrine

Status: active local editorial authority  
Owner: Mike Gilmore

## Purpose

This module governs public portfolio writing, AI-assisted publishing, series continuity, evidence quality, voice, and editorial review. It is a public-content authority and must obey the Master Doctrine's disclosure and professional-read gates.

## Structure

The only living child package is `system/`:

- `AI_ASSISTED_PUBLISHING_WORKFLOW.md` owns the repeatable research, drafting, verification, review and publication workflow.
- `NEXT_SERIES_FOUNDATION.md` owns the current accepted series direction and next-article continuity.

Do not create loose editorial notes, one-file-per-article plans, or parallel style guides. Update these authorities or the article source itself.

## Boundaries

Editorial authority does not override product, architecture, public-disclosure, URL compatibility, release or production-authorization law. Historical article bodies, publication dates and public routes remain protected production content.
