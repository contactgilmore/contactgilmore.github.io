# Brand Brief

Status: **ACTIVE — proven visual direction**  
Validated: 2026-08-08

## Brand expression

ContactGilmore should present Mike Gilmore as an experienced technical delivery and customer-solutions professional with credible systems, implementation, integration, reliability, and program-delivery depth.

The visual expression is:

```text
experienced
calm
technical
customer-aware
modern
precise
credible
```

The site should feel closer to a restrained modern B2B SaaS/product editorial experience than to a conventional résumé page or themed developer portfolio.

## Visual personality

Use high-contrast oversized editorial headlines, disciplined spacing, simple neutral surfaces, restrained blue accents, generous but intentional whitespace, strong evidence cards, and minimal chrome.

The design should create confidence through hierarchy and clarity rather than animation or decorative technology imagery.

## Content-to-design relationship

Professional evidence outranks technology decoration. The visual hierarchy should reinforce this order:

```text
professional identity
proof / outcomes
selected work
technical depth
writing
experience / background
contact
```

Technical tools may appear as concise tags or supporting context. They must not become a giant logo wall or substitute for evidence.

## Tone

Copy and design should feel confident without exaggeration. Avoid generic personal-brand slogans, consultant clichés, buzzword density, fake testimonials, fabricated metrics, skill percentages, or visual devices that imply expertise without proof.

## Brand mark

The compact `MG` monogram is the default small navigation mark. It should remain simple, high-contrast, and subordinate to the content. Do not build the interface around a large personal logo treatment.

## Success condition

A recruiter or hiring manager should be able to understand the professional direction quickly, then move naturally into proof. A technical reader should be able to go deeper without the site changing personality between portfolio and blog surfaces.
